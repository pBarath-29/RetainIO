# Product Walkthrough Playbook

## When To Offer A Walkthrough

Offer a walkthrough when the customer's complaint centers on confusion, misconfiguration, or
unclear product usage rather than a price objection. Common triggers: onboarding confusion, low
feature adoption despite otherwise-active usage, or repeated basic support tickets about the
same feature.

## Walkthrough Versus Engineering Escalation

A walkthrough is appropriate for usage or configuration issues. A technical escalation to
engineering is appropriate for confirmed bugs, outages, or performance problems. If it's unclear
which applies, default to escalation first — walking a customer through a feature that is
actually broken will not land well and can make the relationship worse.

## Scheduling And Preparation

Walkthroughs should be scheduled within three business days of being offered. The assigned CSM
should come prepared with the account's specific SHAP risk factors and recent review
history, so the session addresses the customer's actual pain points rather than a generic tour.
