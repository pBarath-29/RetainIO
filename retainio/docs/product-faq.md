# RetainIO Platform — Product FAQ

## API Timeouts And Rate Limiting

If a customer reports API timeouts or rate limiting, treat this as a technical/product issue,
not a pricing issue. Escalate to Solutions Engineering immediately — a discount does not resolve
an unresolved technical blocker, and historically does not prevent churn in these cases.

## Integration Timelines

Standard REST integrations take one to two weeks. Customers reporting multi-week integration
struggles should be flagged for a dedicated technical walkthrough rather than left to keep
troubleshooting alone.

## Combining Discounts

Discounts cannot be stacked. An existing discount must run out its months or be explicitly
removed before a
new one can be applied to the same account.

## Competitor Price Comparisons

When a customer cites a cheaper competitor, first confirm whether that competitor genuinely has
feature parity. If yes, a matching or near-matching discount is usually warranted. If the
competitor has a narrower feature set, a value-based conversation highlighting the gap may be
more effective than discounting.

## Security And Compliance Reviews

If a customer's security or compliance team is auditing data handling, this is a compliance
concern, not a commercial one. Offer a dedicated security review session and documentation
package — do not offer a discount as a response to a compliance question.

## Sudden Usage Drops

A sudden usage drop can mean disengagement risk, but it can also reflect seasonal variation
(for example, education customers over summer break) or a champion departure. Confirm with a
proactive check-in call before assuming churn risk and taking action.

## What Happens When A Discount Ends

The return to the standard rate is itself a moment of churn risk, and one that is routinely
missed because the account looks healthy while the discount is running. Diarise the end of the
discounted period and make contact before the customer sees the higher invoice, not after.

Do not chain a second discount onto the end of the first to avoid the conversation. Stacking is
prohibited, and an account that has effectively never paid the standard rate has no established
price for the product — every renewal then becomes a negotiation from the discounted number
rather than the list one.

If the account still looks at risk as the discount ends, treat that as a signal the discount
was not addressing the real problem, and check what the risk drivers actually say.
